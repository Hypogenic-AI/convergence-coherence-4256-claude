# Downloaded Papers

## Core Papers: CoT as Dynamical Systems

1. **Metastable Dynamics of Chain-of-Thought Reasoning** (2502.01694_metastable_dynamics_CoT.pdf)
   - Authors: Juno Kim, Denny Wu, Jason D. Lee, Taiji Suzuki
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2502.01694
   - Why relevant: Most directly relevant — models CoT as Markov chain with metastable clusters, proves power-law convergence rate Θ̃(KM/ε)

2. **A Stochastic Dynamical Theory of LLM Self-Adversariality** (2501.16783_stochastic_dynamical_LLM.pdf)
   - Authors: Jack David Carson
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2501.16783
   - Why relevant: SDE/Fokker-Planck framework for CoT dynamics, phase transition analysis, convergence/divergence criteria

3. **A Statistical Physics of Language Model Reasoning** (2506.04374_statistical_physics_LM_reasoning.pdf)
   - Authors: Jack David Carson, Amir Reisizadeh
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2506.04374
   - Why relevant: Empirical validation of SDE framework across 8 models, regime-switching SLDS model, PCA-based dimensionality reduction

4. **SDE Framework for Multi-Objective LLM Interactions** (2510.10739_SDE_framework_LLM.pdf)
   - Authors: Shukla, Joshi
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2510.10739
   - Why relevant: Eigenvalue-based convergence rate classification for iterative LLM processes
